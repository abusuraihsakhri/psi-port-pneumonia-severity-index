Pneumonia Severity Index (PSI / PORT)

Adult community-acquired pneumonia · Two-step Fine/PORT prediction rule

Clinical decision support, not a diagnosis. PSI estimates 30-day mortality risk. It does not determine ICU admission or replace clinical judgment. Urgent respiratory failure, shock, hypoxemia, and other instability require evaluation irrespective of PSI class.

No names, identifiers, or records are needed. Calculations run only in this browser; no patient inputs are sent to a server or saved.

Demographics

The historical score models sex as male or female, with 10 fewer points for female sex. The model is not validated in children or for other sex-variable categories.

Assessment confirmation

If any required finding is unknown, do not use the displayed class to exclude severe pneumonia. Obtain the necessary clinical assessment first.
